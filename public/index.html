Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, Telegram access, privacy, and community rules.

Why are prices listed in both INR and Telegram Stars? ▼
The INR amounts (₹149, ₹499, ₹999) represent our positioning target benchmarks for members in India. Under Telegram's platform policy, in-app digital channel memberships must be billed in Telegram Stars (XTR). When subscribing, you purchase Stars through Apple App Store or Google Play in INR and redeem them for the subscription.
How do I get access to the private Telegram channel? ▼
Once your Telegram Stars payment is successfully confirmed by the Telegram Bot API, our server immediately issues a single-use private channel invite link. Clicking the link admits your verified Telegram account into the channel corresponding to your tier.
How does recurring subscription renewal and cancellation work? ▼
Subscriptions renew automatically every 30 days. You can cancel auto-renewal at any time without fees directly inside the Telegram application by navigating to Settings > Subscriptions. When canceled, your access remains valid until the 30-day period expires.
What happens to my channel access if my subscription expires? ▼
If your subscription expires or renewal fails, our automated entitlement engine revokes channel access. You can resubscribe at any time to regain entry.
Can I get a refund if I change my mind? ▼
Digital memberships paid with Telegram Stars are non-refundable digital goods under Telegram Terms of Service. In cases of duplicate charges or technical failure, you can contact our Grievance Redressal desk.
Who is eligible to join StrangersMeetup? ▼
You must be at least 21 years of age and residing in Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Karnataka, or Kerala. Falsifying age or geographical location is a violation of our Terms of Service.
