Frequently Asked Questions

Answers & Clarifications

Everything you need to know about attendance, privacy, and community etiquette.

Why are venues kept private until 24 hours before?

To protect attendee physical safety, prevent unvetted walk-ins, and ensure an intimate, respectful atmosphere for every gathering.

What happens if I attend alone?

Over 80% of our attendees come solo! Our regional hosts greet you at the entrance and introduce you to an initial conversation circle immediately.

How does Telegram coordination work?

Joining via Telegram keeps your personal phone number confidential. You receive venue coordinates, emergency host contact, and attendee introductions safely.

What is the cancellation policy?

Local meetups offer full refunds up to 24-48 hours prior. Trips have explicit cancellation tiers stated on each itinerary card.
