Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Aligned with Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023

1. Principles of Data Minimization

We strictly practice data minimization. We do not collect Aadhaar numbers, national identity cards, or physical address documents for initial membership. Our age gate collects self-declared date of birth and general territory (State code). We never record or expose precise GPS coordinates in public member channels.

2. Telegram Architecture & Username Privacy

Community participation occurs via official private Telegram channels. By setting your Telegram privacy configuration to hide your phone number from non-contacts, your phone number remains invisible to other community members. We do not inspect or read private direct messages between members on Telegram.

3. Data We Collect

• Account Identifiers: Telegram User ID, Telegram username, declared age, state code.
• Payment Telemetry: Telegram Stars payment transaction identifiers, active subscription period, and renewal timestamps.
• Analytics & Logs: Minimal pseudonymous conversion funnel events, IP address hash, and security audit timestamps.

4. Retention & Right to Erasure

In accordance with Section 12 of the DPDPA 2023, you have the right to request erasure of your personal data when you terminate membership. Payment transaction records are retained solely for the statutory period required under Indian financial and tax regulations.
