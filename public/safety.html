In-Person Dating Safety Guide

Essential protocols for consensual, secure adult meetups.

1. Public Places Only for Initial Encounters

Always choose well-lit, populated public venues for your first several meetings—such as reputable cafes, restaurants, or cultural centers in busy commercial areas. Never agree to meet in isolated parks, private hotel rooms, or residences on early dates.

2. Control Your Transportation

Arrange your own transport to and from the venue (such as your own vehicle or an app-based cab). Avoid having a new acquaintance pick you up at your home or workplace until mutual trust has been firmly established.

3. Inform a Trusted Contact

Always let a friend or family member know where you are going, who you are meeting, and when you expect to return. Consider sharing a live GPS location via messaging apps during the meeting.

4. Guard Your Financial Boundaries

Never transfer money, wire funds, or share bank OTPs with anyone you meet online. Romance scams frequently involve manufactured emergencies, medical distress stories, or travel fund requests.

5. Emergency Helplines in India

  • National Emergency Response System: 112
  • National Women Helpline: 1091 / 181
  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Helpline: 1930
