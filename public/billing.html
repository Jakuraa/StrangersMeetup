Billing & Cancellation Policy

Full transparency on subscription renewal, Telegram Stars, and channel entitlement life cycles.

1. Billing Currency: Telegram Stars (XTR)

All membership tiers are billed exclusively in Telegram Stars (XTR), Telegram's native in-app virtual currency. Listed INR amounts (₹149, ₹499, ₹999) represent domestic target reference pricing. Telegram Stars can be purchased directly within Telegram via the Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) using your preferred local payment methods (UPI, debit card, net banking).

2. 30-Day Recurring Cycle

Memberships operate on a 30-day recurring cycle (2,592,000 seconds). Unless canceled by you, your Telegram account will automatically be billed every 30 days to sustain uninterrupted channel access.

3. How to Cancel Auto-Renewal

You have complete autonomy over your recurring subscription. To cancel:

  1. Open the Telegram app on your mobile device or desktop.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Subscriptions.
  3. Find StrangersMeetup under active subscriptions.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription.

When canceled, your membership remains active until the end of your current paid 30-day term. No subsequent charges will occur.

4. Channel Access Expiration Policy

When your paid subscription period concludes without renewal, your private channel entitlement is automatically revoked by our automated server engine. You are welcome to resubscribe at any time to obtain a fresh invite link.

5. Refunds

Under Telegram's Terms of Service, Telegram Stars transactions for digital channel memberships are considered digital goods and are generally non-refundable once channel access is provisioned.
