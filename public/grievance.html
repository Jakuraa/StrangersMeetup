Statutory Timelines

• Acknowledgment: Every grievance received shall be acknowledged within twenty-four (24) hours.
• Resolution & Disposal: The grievance shall be resolved within a period of fifteen (15) days from the date of its receipt, in accordance with applicable rules.
• Intimate Content Removal: Any complaint regarding non-consensual sharing of intimate images or impersonation shall be acted upon within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt.
